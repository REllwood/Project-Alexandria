# Brief shapes

Every brief is synthesized from the vault and **cites the notes used**. Keep them tight and skimmable; lead with what the reader needs. Flag gaps and offer kb-research rather than guessing.

## Client overview
- **Who they are** — from the client hub + entities.
- **Knowledge bases** — the projects under this client, one line each.
- **Key people** — stakeholders and primary engineers (link person notes).
- **Current state** — recent activity (log/timeline), what's in flight.
- **Recent decisions** — last few `Decisions/` (with status).
- **Open questions / risks** — from `questions.md` and stale flags.

## Meeting prep
- **Attendees** — link person notes; one line on each person's stake/role.
- **Context** — the relevant project state and the last related meeting.
- **Likely topics** — open questions and in-flight decisions touching the attendees.
- **Talking points / asks** — what to confirm or push for.

## Status brief
- **Since last update** — new sources, decisions, people, repo activity (this is essentially a kb-digest framed as prose).
- **On track / at risk** — tie to open questions and blockers.
- **Next** — recommended actions.

## Topic deep-dive
- **What we know about X** — synthesized from the strongest sources, most-credible first.
- **Where it lives** — the notes/Sources that cover it.
- **Unknowns** — gaps; offer kb-research.

## Output conventions
- Citations as wikilinks to the exact notes (`[[…/Sources/foo]]`).
- If saving, write to `<KB>/briefs/<slug>.md` with `type: source`? No — use `type: concept` or a dedicated `brief` tag; link from `_index.md`.
- For a polished doc, hand to kb-export (docx/pdf).
